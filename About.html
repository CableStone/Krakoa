Collection One — Choshen

Twelve tribes, read in stone and color.

The Choshen Collection takes the high priest's breastplate as its color card. Twelve pieces, one for each tribe, each garment-dyed to its own stone — odem for Yehuda, sapir for Levi, yashfeh for Binyamin — with the tribe's mark embroidered small and low.

Collection Two — Tzadik Him

The faces we keep close.

Tzadik Him puts hand-drawn portraits of the tzadikim on heavyweight cotton — tees, crewnecks, hoodies. Each portrait is drawn in the studio, printed in small runs, and treated with the weight it deserves: no slogans, no noise, just the face.
